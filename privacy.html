Privacy & analytics

Private messages

Messages are sent only to the project lead to respond to your inquiry and are never displayed publicly. Your optional name, email, area of interest, message and consent are used to handle your request. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information. Messages are retained for up to 365 days and can be deleted earlier. To request deletion, email wyonliu@gmail.com.

Anonymous aggregate analytics

We aggregate pageviews, video plays, experience interactions, resource opens and visits to the contact section by language and a limited set of source labels. Pageviews are not unique people. No cookies or persistent visitor identifiers are used, and messages, email addresses and full referrer URLs are never sent to analytics. Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) disable analytics. Network services may retain necessary connection details in security logs; these are not used as public visitor metrics.

Local storage saves only your opt-out preference. Clearing browser data removes this preference.
