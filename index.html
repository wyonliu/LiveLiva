THE INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

Lives with memory.
Worlds with consequences.

Persistent participation requires more than generation: understanding, action, rules and learning must work together.

I

Personal intelligence

Identity, memory and context inform proposed actions. People can correct the AI’s understanding and control what it may do.

NUWA / IDENTITY · MEMORY · INTENT
II

Spatial & world intelligence

Generate interactive scenes and predict consequences. A world runtime enforces permissions and rules, updating shared state from actual outcomes.

PANGU + FUXI / CREATE · ACT · VERIFY
III

Continual learning & RSI

Research capability retention and transfer, and improvements to the learning process itself. Test gains on held-out tasks, under equal budgets, with rollback.

RESEARCH / RETAIN · TRANSFER · IMPROVE

Model predictions remain distinct from recorded events. Technical ambition remains distinct from current evidence.

CURRENT STAGE

Local protocol tests with synthetic accounts cover identity continuity, state conflicts, permission revocation and restart recovery. Human use, cross-world learning gains and independent creator supply remain to be validated.

A PLATFORM FOR HUMAN CREATION

Worlds worth making.
Lives worth sharing.

The culture of a place. The imagination of an author. The everyday life of a community. Each can become a world people participate in.

Start with an experience worth returning to. Then support a second world from an independent author, testing whether persistent AI lives and human relationships motivate participation. Proposed revenue includes paid experiences, subscriptions and expressive items, with creator revenue sharing.
