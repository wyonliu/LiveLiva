LIVELIVA / OCTOBER 2026

Product & technology blueprint

A public summary of the target design. Architecture and product intent are distinguished from validated results.

The product contract

Creators contribute playable worlds rooted in places, culture and imagination. Players explore with friends and persistent AI companions. Identity and valid history continue across worlds; capabilities transfer under world-specific rules and private memories remain permissioned.

The system

Nuwa powers the persistent AI life; Fuxi validates actions and maintains shared world state; Pangu supports creation, testing and publishing. A human model informs what may matter to a person. A world model predicts possible consequences, while the runtime confirms outcomes.

The learning agenda

Memory updates, skill learning and improvements to the learning method are distinct. The proposed research path uses authorized data, held-out tasks, equal evaluation budgets, retention and transfer checks, and rollback. RSI gains have not been established.

The validation milestone

The next milestone pairs a first compelling adventure with a second independent author’s world. It tests whether people return, play together repeatedly and find value in continuity—and whether a creator can produce and operate a world at viable cost.

Evidence scope

Based on the local shared-space acceptance record reviewed on October 3, 2026: two synthetic accounts exercised real local APIs for identity continuity, conflicting actions, permission revocation and restart recovery, with zero model calls. Native client checks separately used protocol simulations. These records do not establish human retention, device experience, continuous cloud operation or learning gains. This is a record summary; the checks were not rerun during this website build.
